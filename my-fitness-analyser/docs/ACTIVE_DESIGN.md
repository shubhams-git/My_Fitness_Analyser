# Field Notes: training in motion

Owner feedback, 5 November 2026: retain the premium quality, replace the hotel-like
royal treatment with an unmistakably active fitness application.

The defining visual is the athlete's route and recorded effort, connected through
one replay and inspection cursor. A compact session workspace replaces the slow
folio rhythm. Left-aligned condensed athletic headlines, large stable figures,
and precise UI typography establish energy without sacrificing legibility.

Palette: carbon #080E16, technical surface #111C29, raised #1C2B3C,
ice #F2F7FC, signal orange #FFAE89, glacier #91DCFA. Ambient cobalt #224CA0
and ember #8F432D are background light, never chart evidence. Orange identifies
actions/route/pace; glacier identifies HR. Semantic warning/error stay separate.

Type: self-hosted Barlow Condensed bold/italic for display and measurements;
Manrope for controls, supporting copy and forms. Font source and OFL:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed

Composition: session title/actions + route replay with actual pace profile;
essential metrics; concise session finding; linked chart/route/time blocks;
four-answer check-in. The memorable moment is a route drawn as a performance
trace, with a marker and elapsed/pace readout responding to replay or inspection.
No invented live workout, achievements, readiness, track geometry or terrain.

UI/UX Pro Max returned fitness-appropriate condensed typography and energetic
contrast. Its marketing funnel, gamification and scroll-snap suggestions are not
applicable to this activity tool. Retain native scroll and explicit replay.
Frontend-design critique: a dark/orange palette alone is generic; the actual
route, sample-aligned pace trace and shared cursor provide the product identity.

Acceptance: fitness identity in the first viewport; check-in reachable there;
replay/inspection functional; missing samples remain gaps; static reduced-motion
alternative; readable controls and 44px targets; phone through desktop layouts.
